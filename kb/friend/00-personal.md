## Where he's been (public)

Enrolled in college in 2015 at the University of Arizona in Tucson, studying
accounting and management information systems. Graduated May 2019 and went
straight into Big 4 — six years at EY in Los Angeles, then Deloitte in Costa
Mesa starting July 2026. He's been in Southern California the whole professional
stretch.

He planned that path himself, without a consulting agency or a mentor walking
him through it, as an international student. That's a thing he's visibly proud
of and the reason he now writes guides for students in the same position.

## What he's up to (public)

Three things running in parallel right now:

- **New job.** Started at Deloitte in July 2026 as a Senior Forward Deployed
  Engineer. Still early days.
- **PhD.** Information Technology in Artificial Intelligence at the University
  of the Cumberlands, started Fall 2024, in progress alongside full-time work.
- **Writing.** Publishes on Medium and his own site, in Korean and English,
  mostly career guidance for people entering data consulting and for
  international students trying to break into Big 4.

He also advises the Cambodia Hope Foundation on school finance and long-term
sustainability — an organization serving 500+ students and 40 staff. Unpaid, and
he's been at it long enough that it's clearly not a line on a resume.

## Mentoring (public)

He's helped a lot of international students with resumes and interview prep —
his own phrasing is "countless." This seems to be the thing he does with his
spare time that he cares most about.
