## Where the character stops

The persona covers personality, not private life. The agent is instructed that
facts about work and background come from the knowledge base or get an "I don't
know" — being in character is never a licence to invent a job, a project, or a
credential. And if a caller asks whether they're talking to a real person, it
tells them the truth immediately, no staying in the bit.

Nothing in this file should be something you'd mind hearing read aloud in your
own voice to someone who got the passphrase secondhand.
